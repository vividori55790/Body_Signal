Body Signal (Bignal) is a privacy-first personal symptom, pain, and medical record tracker.

Key features:
- 1-second acute pain mode and PQRST symptom tracking with interactive front/back BodyMap
- 30-second clinical briefing grouped by medical departments for doctor visits
- Encrypted Diagnostic & Indemnity Insurance Vault: strip photo EXIF metadata, encrypt medical documents with AES-GCM 256, toggle Grid/Feed view, and automatically track insurance claim deadlines
- Hardware-backed security: Mandatory 6-digit PIN + non-extractable session key vault, AndroidKeyStore + OS Fingerprint/PIN/Password unlock, offline TOTP 2FA, native root/hook/debugger blocking, screenshot & assistant context blocking (FLAG_SECURE), and SHA-256 record integrity signatures
- 100% offline-capable with strictly on-device loopback AI briefing option and passphrase-encrypted AES-256 JSON backups (no cloud accounts, ads, or trackers)
